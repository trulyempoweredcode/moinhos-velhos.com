Exclusive hire

The practical details, in one place

This page supports organisers planning a retreat, training or workshop at Moinhos Velhos — pricing, capacity and policies, clarified before you book. Exclusive hire means your group has sole use of the property and facilities for the duration of your stay.

Standard hire includes accommodation for up to 15 overnight guests, and three additional compact single rooms can raise that to 18. All overnight guests — including organisers, facilitators, chefs and support staff — count toward the agreed numbers.

Accommodation

  • 3 premium single rooms
  • 4 twin rooms — 8 beds in total
  • 2 twin cottages — 4 beds, an individual bathroom for each occupant
  • 3 compact single rooms to extend capacity from 15 to 18 (€50 per room per night)
  • A complimentary single room for the retreat host, with a private bathroom next door

Every room and cottage is en suite. Room allocation is managed by you, the retreat organiser.

Minimum hire

A minimum hire fee equivalent to three nights applies to every booking. Shorter stays can be agreed in advance, with the three-night minimum fee still applying.

Check-in & check-out

Guests check in from 4 pm and out by 12 pm. Alternative timings for facilitators and organisers can be agreed in advance, subject to availability.

Catering, optional

The venue is offered on a space-only basis. Where available, our preferred in-house and local retreat chefs can cater at €40–45 per person per day, with a €500 minimum daily spend, subject to chef availability. You are equally welcome to bring your own caterer — we keep a list of recommended local chefs.
