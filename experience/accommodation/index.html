Where you’ll stay

Accommodation & facilities

Six kinds of quiet

Premium singles to canvas under the stars

From the converted mill to shared twin cottages and summer bell tents, every option is built around the same essentials: a real bed, a bathroom close by, and views you’ll want to sit with for a while.

  • All rooms en suite, with wifi and mosquito nets as standard
  • Shared twins and cottages are matched by sex for solo guests
  • Couples and friends can also book a shared room together

The buildings

Twelve buildings, one hillside

Every room sits within a short walk of the temple, the pool and the dining room — pick the one that matches how much company you want.

The Mill

The original water mill, converted into two premium en suite singles — the quietest address on site.

Main Guesthouse

Three twin rooms and a premium single, all en suite, opening onto a wide terrace with garden views.

Twin Cottages

Two timber cottages, each with its own pair of en suite bathrooms behind a dividing curtain for privacy.

The Lodge

Compact en suite singles, simpler than our other rooms but closer to the main house and the pool.

The Temple Room

A shared twin set apart beside the yoga temple — a favourite with anyone who wants a quieter corner of the valley.

Glamping

Bell tents pitched in the grounds from June to September, with electricity, wifi and fans included.

On site

Everything the programme needs, and then some

The Yoga Temple

Forty pictures of ascended masters look on as you practise — guests often mention the strong, settled energy inside. Along with the juice kitchen, it's the heartbeat of the site.

Salt-Water Pool

A 12 x 6 m chemical-free pool, used year round. A conversion unit splits the salt into natural chlorine and oxygen, so the water feels closer to silk than swimming-pool.

The Therapy Clinic

Three treatment rooms at the heart of the site. The QRS electromagnetic bed and parasite-zapping equipment are free to use whenever you like.

Finnish Sauna

Wood-fired and set right beside the pool, with gender-specific slots and mixed sessions too — a favourite pairing on cooler evenings.

Gardens & Greenhouse

Terraced, organic and productive — including a 160 m² greenhouse across twelve buildings and sixteen hectares.

Main Building

A dining room, a dedicated juice kitchen, and a cosy guest living room with a wood stove for cooler nights.
